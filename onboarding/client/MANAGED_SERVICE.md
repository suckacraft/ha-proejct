## How updates work

We never apply updates automatically. Every Home Assistant update is:

1. Tested on our own home system first
2. Held for 48 hours to confirm stability
3. Applied to a small group of client systems
4. Applied to your system after confirmed stability

You will be notified before any update is applied to your system.
If an update causes an issue, we roll back to the previous working state
automatically using your most recent backup.
